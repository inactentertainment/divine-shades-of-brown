# DSB Movies: sourcing and verification audit

Checked 12 October 2026. This is a DSB editorial selection, not a copied ranking or a claim of universal consensus. The canon contains exactly 100 uniquely identified films, ranked consecutively. The separate Recent & Noteworthy shelf contains four released films, not predictions or unverified upcoming releases.

## Reference lists consulted

- [Black Movie Hall of Fame: 100 Best Films](https://blackmoviehalloffame.org/bmhof-100-best-films/) — historical landmarks and American Black screen traditions. Its order was not reused.
- [Rotten Tomatoes: Movies That Celebrate Black Joy](https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/guide/movies-that-celebrate-black-joy/) — helped counter an exclusive focus on oppression through friendship, imagination and creative ambition.
- [BFI: 10 great black British films](https://www.bfi.org.uk/lists/10-great-black-british-films) — British diasporic breadth and overlooked voices.
- [AAFCA / Rotten Tomatoes: Important Films About the Black Experience](https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/guide/important-films-about-the-black-experience/) — supplementary cross-check of landmark selections.

These references informed the selection, not the original commentary. The why, two short lesson paragraphs and question for each film were written specifically for DSB. They are critical viewing prompts rather than exhaustive research essays or a claim to replace the film. Black cinema is defined inclusively here: work by Black filmmakers and Black-centered stories, including some films directed by non-Black filmmakers. Some intense crime classics appear for historical and critical study; their entries discuss consequences, accountability and the risk of glamorizing harm.

## Trailer verification

All 100 canon records and four recent-release records contain real 11-character YouTube video IDs. IDs were retrieved from public YouTube search results, then checked against a live YouTube oEmbed response to match the title and identify the publisher. Each record carries verifiedSource, trailerTitle, channel and publisherUrl so the audit is reproducible. Manual checks corrected collisions with unrelated films named Babylon, Pressure, Dakan, Soul Food and Shaft, plus a misleading match for 13th and Malcolm X.

Recognized studios, distributors and film institutions were preferred. Other records retain identified third-party archival/trailer publishers. Neither a publisher name nor a Black-focused channel title proves Black ownership. No Black-owned-channel claim is made. A publisher named for Blitz Bazawule is likewise not treated as independently authenticated ownership.

IMPORTANT: oEmbed confirms live metadata, not authorization, geographic playback, age restrictions or permission to embed. Actual browser playback must still be checked. Do not automatically redirect an unavailable film to YouTube search. Show an on-site unavailable message and offer the explicit user-controlled Open on YouTube action where appropriate. The catalog includes one official red-band Girls Trip trailer; age/content handling should be clear in the interface.

## Dates and scope

Film years generally identify original film/festival dates rather than restoration or later commercial release. Pressure premiered in 1975, although catalogs also use 1976; Fast Color premiered in 2018 and opened theatrically in 2019. Small Axe: Lovers Rock is a standalone film within an anthology. The recent shelf uses Sing Sing's 2023 premiere, not its wider 2024 theatrical rollout.
