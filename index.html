DSB SELECT · BLACK ATLAS

Not every listing.
A list worth trusting.

DSB Select is our curated layer inside Black Atlas. We look for Black-owned businesses with strong public ratings, meaningful customer feedback, consistent quality and an active reputation. Inclusion is considered, not automatic, and businesses cannot pay their way onto the Select list.

4.0+ public ratingA strong rating is the starting point, not the finish line.
Meaningful review historyWe consider the amount and consistency of customer feedback, not just the star number.
Black ownershipOwnership should be supportable through the business itself or reputable public sources.
Current + activeThe business should be operating, reachable and serving customers now.
Quality + reputationService, product quality, community standing and repeated positive themes all matter.

Own or love a business that belongs here? Send it for consideration. DSB reviews the business independently; submission does not guarantee inclusion.

Public ratings are snapshots and can change. DSB Select is editorial curation, not a warranty or guarantee of any transaction.
