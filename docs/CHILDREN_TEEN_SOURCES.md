# DSB Teen Years 100 — curation and sources

Prepared 12 October 2026. Ranks, suggested ages, reading-demand labels, and family activities are DSB editorial judgments, not assessments, Lexile scores, grade-equivalence claims, or a vote result. The list is a guided sequence balancing joy, identity, imagination, civic understanding, STEM, and difficult histories. Mature titles carry content notes; adults should preview them.

Source fields point to primary publishers, author catalogs, professional author bodies, or public-library records. Some sources are author bibliographies rather than individual product pages. Publication years refer to first publication of the selected work, not the newest paperback or regional reprint. ISBNs appear only where verified in a retrieved publisher URL; no ISBNs or cover IDs were invented.

Nonfiction STEM selections include mathematicians’ histories and memoirs; the shelf is not a mathematics curriculum. Science-tagged fiction provides conversations about technology and curiosity, not scientific instruction. Black-led memoir collaborations explicitly retain all coauthor credits, including Andrew Aydin and illustrator Nate Powell.
