## Scope and exceptions

- The free button should open the provider in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`. These are original-work editions, not a promise of current publisher introductions, annotations, or cover artwork.
- Passing is delivered inside Standard Ebooks’ Short Fiction collection, which explicitly includes the full novella. Its edition page supplies EPUB/Kindle/Kobo downloads and online reading. The interface should show that provider/collection context. A university alternative is [Lehigh’s full text of Passing](https://scalar.lehigh.edu/african-american-poetry-a-digital-anthology/nella-larsen-passing-1929-full-text-of-novel), also verified as the full 1929 novel.
- No other eligible pre-1931 titles are missing from the existing canon. Later works were not assumed public domain from age or from finding a PDF online.
- Selected Poems of Langston Hughes (1959) is a particular collection; free earlier Hughes poems do not make that whole collection a legal free edition.
- The Mis-Education of the Negro (1933), Black Reconstruction in America (1935), and Their Eyes Were Watching God (1937) are beyond the pre-1931 U.S. publication-date cutoff in 2026. No blanket free-edition claim is made for them.
- Rights vary by country. The visible note should read: “Free edition available in the U.S.; check local copyright rules elsewhere.”
